Responsible Use

Use safely. Grow confidently.

Every pack carries a toxicity label and directions for use. Please follow them for the best results and safety.

01

Read the label

Check the crop, target pest, dose and waiting period printed on the pack before use.

02

Wear protection

Use gloves, a mask and full-sleeve clothing while mixing and spraying.

03

Spray right

Spray in calm weather, avoid drift and keep children and animals away.

04

Store & dispose

Keep packs locked away from food. Never reuse empty containers.
